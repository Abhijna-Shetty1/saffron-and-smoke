Our Philosophy

Fire is our first ingredient.

We believe Rajasthani food doesn't need to be reinvented. It needs to be respected, understood and given room to speak.

Our kitchen works with slow cooking, seasonal produce, hand-ground spices and the unmistakable warmth of food cooked over fire.
